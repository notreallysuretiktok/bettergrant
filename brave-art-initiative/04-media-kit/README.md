# Media Kit — Drew "Bullet" Matthews

Pulled from the podcast guest one-sheet. Use this for podcast bookings, press, and the website's founder page.

## One-line

Army veteran and founder of Brave Art Initiative, helping people turn pain into purpose through art, storytelling, and real conversation.

## Signature quote

> "I didn't find peace when I came home. I had to build it."

## Background

- Army veteran, sniper, combat deployment
- Army Commendation Medal recipient
- Pastor's kid who questioned everything early
- Two divorces, hard lessons, real growth
- Lost his identity after the military
- Motorcycle club life, searching for brotherhood
- Chop shop past, took accountability, served time
- Coped the wrong way: alcohol, chaos, survival mode

## Core story

Went from high-level purpose in the military to coming home completely lost. Didn't understand what combat, blasts, stress, and survival mode had done to his nervous system. Coped the wrong way and made mistakes, and owns that. What he learned: **no matter how far off track you get, you can rebuild.**

## Mission now

- Helping people find their voice through art, storytelling, and real conversation
- Creating space for people to express what they can't always say out loud
- Breaking cycles, especially as a father
