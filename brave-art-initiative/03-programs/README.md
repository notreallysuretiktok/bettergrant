## Program ideas pulled from the mission

From the podcast one-sheet: *"Helping people find their voice through art, storytelling, and real conversation."*

- **Story to Canvas**: participants turn one moment from their story into a piece of art
- **Real Conversations**: a podcast or live-recorded talks with veterans and survivors
- **Breaking Cycles**: art sessions for parents and kids together
- **Book projects**: helping people write and illustrate their own story, the way the BAI project covers were made
